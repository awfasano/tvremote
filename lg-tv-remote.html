LG TV remote app

An LG TV remote that lives on your phone.

TV Remote connects to LG webOS TVs over your Wi-Fi, with no account, no ads and no weekly fee.

Free for one TV. Pro is $9.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $4.99/year.

LG support is new and marked Beta in the app. If your model has trouble, email awfasano@gmail.com with the model and we'll fix it.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Turn the TV on and put your device on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open TV Remote, tap Find My TV and choose your LG.
  3. The TV asks you to accept the connection. Accept it once.

What you can do

  • The D-pad, OK, Back and Home
  • Volume, mute and power off
  • Play, pause, rewind and fast-forward
  • Open your TV apps (Pro)
  • Type with your keyboard (Pro)

Questions

The app can't find my LG

Check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi. On some webOS versions a setting called LG Connect Apps must be on; it's under Network or General, depending on the model.

I don't see the pairing prompt

Look at the TV right after choosing it in the app. The prompt only appears for a short time, so try again if you missed it.
