TV Remote privacy

Your TVs. Your network. No servers.

TV Remote controls the TVs on your own Wi-Fi. It has no accounts, no developer-run server, no advertising, and no tracking or analytics SDK. It never asks you to sign in.

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Applies to TV Remote for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and this website.

How it controls your TV

TV Remote finds TVs on the local network you're connected to — using Bonjour, a scan of your own Wi-Fi subnet, or an IP address you type in — and sends your button presses straight to the TV over that network. Those commands go device-to-TV; they do not pass through any server we run.

What's stored, and where

The TVs you save (a name, local IP address and brand) and your recently used apps are kept in the app's own on-device storage so the remote is ready next time. Where a TV requires pairing, the pairing token it issues is stored in your device's Keychain, keyed to that TV. None of this is uploaded to us or synced to a developer service. Device backup may include app data depending on your system settings. Removing the app removes this data from the device.

Local network permission

iOS asks your permission for TV Remote to find and connect to devices on your local network. The app uses that access only to discover and control your TVs. It does not scan for or report anything else, and nothing from your network leaves your home.

Purchases

Pro is an optional in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. The full remote for one TV is free. Pro is available as a one-time purchase ($9.99) or an auto-renewing yearly plan ($4.99/year) that begins with a 7-day free trial — cancel during the trial and you are not charged. Apple processes payment and manages the subscription; we never see or receive your card or payment details. Manage or cancel a subscription any time in your App Store account settings.

Support email

If you email awfasano@gmail.com, we receive your address, your message and anything you choose to attach, and use it only to answer you and fix the issue. Please don't send sensitive information unless it's needed. Ask by email if you'd like a support conversation deleted.

This website

This is a static site with no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or sign-up forms. The hosting provider may receive ordinary request information such as IP address and browser details to serve and secure the pages. Email links open your mail app; they do not submit a form here.

Children

TV Remote is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children, and it does not collect personal information.

Changes and contact

We'll update this policy if these practices change. For any privacy question, email awfasano@gmail.com with "TV Remote" in the subject.
