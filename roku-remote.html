Roku remote app

A Roku remote on your iPhone. No subscription.

TV Remote finds your Roku TV or Roku player on your Wi-Fi and gives you the whole remote: the D-pad, Home and Back, playback, volume and your channels. No account, no ads, no weekly fee.

Free for one TV. Pro is $9.99 once, or a 7-day free trial then $4.99/year.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Put your iPhone, iPad or Mac on the same Wi-Fi as your Roku.
  2. Open TV Remote and tap Find My TV. Your Roku shows up by name.
  3. Tap it. Roku needs no code or approval, so you're in control right away.

What you can do

  • The D-pad, OK, Back and Home
  • Play, pause, rewind and fast-forward
  • Open your channels: Netflix, YouTube, Disney+ and the rest (Pro)
  • Type searches with your keyboard instead of the on-screen letters (Pro)
  • Volume and power. Roku TVs handle them directly; Roku sticks pass them to your TV over HDMI-CEC
  • On a Mac, the arrow keys, Return and Space work as the remote

Questions

My Roku says it's blocking remote control apps

Your Roku's network access is set to Limited. Open Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps › Network access and choose Default. TV Remote still handles the main buttons in Limited mode.

Power or volume doesn't work with my Roku stick

A stick reaches your TV's power and volume through HDMI-CEC. Turn on CEC in your TV's settings. Samsung calls it Anynet+, LG calls it SIMPLINK, Sony calls it Bravia Sync.

Does it work with Roku Express, Streaming Stick and Ultra?

Yes. Any Roku player or Roku TV on your Wi-Fi works, including Roku TVs from TCL, Hisense and others.
