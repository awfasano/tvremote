Common questions

The app can't find my TV

Make sure the TV is powered on and on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone (not a guest network, and not 5GHz vs 2.4GHz on separate SSIDs). Allow the Local Network permission when iOS asks — it's required to reach your TV. If it still doesn't appear, tap Add TV and enter the TV's IP address, which you'll find in the TV's network settings or your router.

My Roku says it's blocking control

On the Roku, open Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps › Network access and set it to Default (or Permissive). TV Remote also works around Roku's Limited mode automatically where it can.

Power or volume doesn't work on a streaming stick

A streaming stick (like a Roku or Fire TV stick) reaches the TV's power and volume through HDMI-CEC. Turn CEC on in your TV's settings — it's called Anynet+ (Samsung), SIMPLINK (LG), Bravia Sync (Sony), or just CEC — then try again.

Fire TV asks for a PIN

That's expected the first time. Enter the PIN shown on the TV screen; the pairing is remembered after that.

A brand is marked "Beta"

Those TV types work in our testing but we haven't confirmed them on every model yet. If yours misbehaves, email us the brand and model and we'll get it working.

I paid before and want it back

Open the app's Go Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Pro restores on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Managing or cancelling the yearly plan

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Cancel during the trial and you're never charged. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.
